DreamerV3 sees a 64 × 64 image and learns an internal model from experience. Its policy learns through imagined sequences. This small run uses a 687,875-parameter model and trains on the Spark’s GPU.

The reconstruction view decodes the model’s internal state after seeing the current image. It is a real neural-network output, and may be blurry or inaccurate. It is not a prediction of the future.

The record includes every evaluation seed and the matching random-action comparisons. This short run is not the full Crafter benchmark.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF CHALLENGE

Find water. Make tools.
Survive the night.

Crafter packs exploration, resource collection, crafting, and combat into one small survival game. Longer training can pursue a chain of skills, from collecting wood to making tools and mining rarer materials.

This notebook captures an early checkpoint. The saved setup can resume training on the Spark, then export new episodes for the site.
