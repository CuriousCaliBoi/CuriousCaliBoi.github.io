About this collection

A microphone in Fremont records bird calls. BirdNET suggests the species; these are sound-based identifications, not confirmed sightings. Matches of at least 75% confidence are shown by default.

The portraits are illustrations, not photographs of the recorded birds. Each entry links to Cornell’s identification guide.
