A little portrait album of the voices outside. These are imagined AI watercolors, paired with real listening records. Open a bird to wander through its days in the garden.

About the watercolors

These AI illustrations were painted with MiniT2I on a DGX Spark. They imagine an adult bird; a recorded call doesn’t tell us its age, sex, or plumage. The paintings can miss identifying marks, so each portrait links to the Cornell Lab’s photographs and field notes.

New species join the collection as they’re heard, even before their portrait is ready. Finished watercolors are saved here, ready to enjoy whenever you visit.
