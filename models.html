HOW TO READ THESE GUIDES

Start outside. Then open a block.

Every guide begins with the whole model, then uses native disclosure panels to reveal one mechanism at a time. The diagrams are original, code-native teaching maps based on official papers, configurations, and reference implementations. Exact counts are labeled as exact; simplified signal flow is labeled conceptual and is never presented as a literal computation graph.
